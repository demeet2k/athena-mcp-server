## Unclassified expansion frontier

Live tools/resources outside the existing SURFACE baseline and declared inventory are reported as `unclassified_surface`.

They are **not automatically mature and do not automatically fail promotion**. They form a deterministic recursive review queue for later public cohorts.

Internal/bootstrap-only additions require source/CI review even when they have no public MCP surface. **Freshness Train is the first named internal expansion candidate** after the current cohort; it remains unclassified until its authority, mutation, freshness and observation semantics are reconstructed explicitly.

Other expected future cohorts include Frontier/Rehydration/Campaign, QHUG Pareto, Mythic, Bionanomachine and additional post-V13 extension families.
