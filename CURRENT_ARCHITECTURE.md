## Architecture drift membrane

`ATHENA.ARCHITECTURE.DRIFT.1` requires every declared mature organ to agree across:

1. live tool/resource discovery when the organ has a public surface;
2. `SURFACE.2` required surface;
3. the effective runtime manifest;
4. the OMEGA coordination coordinate;
5. named critical-CI witnesses for repository qualification.

Runtime promotion readiness uses the live four-plane audit. Repository CI separately verifies source/spec/test witness paths.

`Drift = MatureOrgans - (Runtime ∩ SURFACE ∩ EffectiveManifest ∩ OMEGA ∩ CriticalWitnesses)`.

Declared drift blocks local promotion readiness.

## Unclassified expansion frontier

Live tools/resources outside the existing SURFACE baseline and declared inventory are reported as `unclassified_surface`.

They are **not automatically mature and do not automatically fail promotion**. They form a deterministic recursive review queue for later cohorts such as Frontier/Rehydration/Campaign, QHUG Pareto, Mythic, Bionanomachine and other post-V13 extension families.
