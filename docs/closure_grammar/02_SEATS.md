## Extra: the witness and the uninvited

The commonest seat, and the one the oracle devices make physical.

- **Set aside before the count.** One yarrow stalk of fifty (the reading is done with 7² = 49); the seventeenth ikin kept apart 🟡; two extra cowries beside the sixteen 🟢; the mālā's guru bead "not used for counting … rather than crossing it the mālā is turned" 🟢; the misbaḥa's imām bead; the Reconciler of the geomantic shield; the joker.
- **Not numbered.** The Fool ("unnumbered, sometimes 0 or XXII"); Da'at ("not a sefirah … an empty slot"; Cordovero counts Keter and omits Da'at, Luria the reverse — the eleventh is never seated); the Golden Dawn Neophyte 0°=0□ "outside the Tree" and the unnumbered Portal; Thelema's unnumbered grade at every threshold; the Scottish Rite's honorary 33rd; "the Levites were not numbered among them" (Num 1:47); Enochian "18 explicit calls + 1 implicit".
- **The uninvited one breaks the system.** Eris, the one goddess not asked to the wedding, throws the apple; Ọ̀ṣun, the one female among the irúnmọlẹ̀, is excluded and "the world fails" until she is admitted; Loki arrives uninvited at Ægir's feast (the "thirteenth guest" count is *not* in the sources 🟡); the Black Brothers who "build a false sefira in Daath … become trapped" — occupying the excluded slot is the named failure. Ω43's reading ("the system does not run without its crossing element") now has four independent witnesses.
- **The hidden one above.** The hundredth name "added as the highest name" to the 99; Atum over the Ogdoad; the Kheper Ganitam's "hidden 13th … that commutes with all 12 hours"; Thoth who "does not cast a vote" over the 42; the dog as "the fourth … the sixth … the eighth" of the Sleepers (Q 18:22) — the guardian animal is the n+1 at every count the verse tries.
- **The witness.** Puruṣa, "the twenty-fifth tattva … the witness-consciousness" that does not act; the Sufi *muraqabah al-Raqīb*, "watching the Watcher"; Orunmila "witness of destiny"; Osun the watchman-staff as the fourth Warrior 🟠; the seventeenth nut. The (n+1)th is exactly the one that observes and is not counted.
- **The gate (n = 0).** Where the count has not begun, the crossing element is the gate itself: Legba "always saluted first … when I return I will salute the Lwa"; Èṣù "without whom nothing moves", carved at the head of the tray; Elegguá "behind the door"; Janus; the crossroads with its "don't look back". The registry writes these as 0|1.

## The functional seat: why the extra one exists

Ω2.1 adds the systems that *say why*. An Athenian public jury sits 501 (or 201, 401) because 'odd numbers prevented deadlocks': the (n+1)th member exists so that a closed body can decide. The Great Sanhedrin sits 71 because 'seventy elders … plus Moses himself' — the presiding one over the round count. A jury of twelve keeps alternates who 'do not take part in deliberating': witnesses to the count who are not of it. A parity bit is added to seven data bits and 'carries no data'; a check digit is appended 'so that a computer can quickly check for errors'; the leap second is written 23:59:60, counted once and dropped. These are the witness, the tie-breaker and the checksum — three engineering names for one seat — and they are what the Kheper Ganitam meant when it called 1/64 'the axiom added to the system to make it consistent'.

## Centre: four and the middle

Where the closed count is a ring of directions, the (n+1)th is at the centre and is usually the seat of the observer, the ruler, or the axis.

Mide, "the middle", the fifth of Ireland's "fifths" 🟢; the Lakota's six directions (four cardinal, earth, sky) with 'the centre point completing a seventh' 🟢 — the only 6 + centre in the registry, a native derivation of 7 from the directions of space; Cusco at the meeting of the four suyus 🟢; the Aztec navel; Earth at the centre of the four phases and 5 at the centre of the Lo Shu 🟢; Vairocana among the four Buddha families 🟢; the Kongo dikenga's crossing point 🟢; the Plains wheel's cairn 🟢; the poto mitan 🟢; the Enochian Tablet of Union "the fifth element" binding four watchtowers, with one large Sigillum for the centre and four small for the legs 🟢; the Golden Dawn hexagram of six planets "with the Sun at the centre" 🟢; the Go board's tiānyuán over 360 🟡; Dao Su's "(½,½,½), equidistant from all eight trigrams, the Taiji core around which the eight states rotate" 🟠; the Sufi quṭb among the forty abdāl 🟡. The Vedic corpus document puts the observer here explicitly: "12 outer generators (the rāśis), 1 central charge (the lagna / observer)".

The two vigesimal cultures seat it the same way at the scale of 400: the four hundred Huitznahua and their one sister; the four hundred irúnmọlẹ̀ and one.

## Withdrawn: the last one hidden

Here the nominal count is n+1 but the last member is withheld, and the tradition says so.

The twelfth Imam in occultation since 941 (the Seveners hide the seventh); 'fifty gates of understanding were created in the world, and all of them were given to Moses, except for one' (Rosh Hashanah 21b) 🟢; the Etruscan nomen's eight saecula with the ninth as its end 🟡; three of the twelve Nephite disciples who 'tarry' while nine depart 🟢; Sophia, "lowest/last" of the thirty, who acts without her consort and produces the residue; Odin's eighteenth charm, "which I never will tell"; Terminus, the boundary stone that would not move when the new temple was built; the aleph of EMET whose erasure leaves MET; the sixteenth kalā of the moon that never wanes 🟡; citrinitas, the third of four alchemical stages "often merged" into the fourth; Enoch, the seventh, who "was not". The Enochian Abyss is the tenth aethyr *inside* the thirty — the crossing can sit inside the count when the count is a descent.

The Egyptian corpus document states it as physics: "the sun that rises in the east is not a new sun; it is the old sun with its entropy reset to zero."

## Residue: the leftover declared

The year: 5 epagomenal (Egypt, Zoroaster, Mandaea, Maya, Aztec, the French Republic's sansculottides), 4 added days 'named instead of numbered' (Enoch), 4/5 Ayyám-i-Há (Baháʼí), Year Day outside any week (the International Fixed Calendar), St. Tib's Day outside week and season (Discordian), the Igbo's one day over 13 × 28, 2 months in 5 years (Coligny), 7 months in 19 (Babylon, China, Israel, India), 61 uncounted winter days (Romulan Rome), 37 days of the Pleiades' invisibility (Andean 🟡), 13 days by which nine Akan cycles overshoot. The measure: 1/64 of the Eye 🟡; the leimma and the comma; the 14th piece of Osiris replaced by a made one; the Lost Word. The oracle: yarrow remainders removed; invalid grabs of 0 or ≥3 nuts; the rainbow body's "hair and nails". Where a tradition names its residue it has told you where it does not close; this is the corpus's invariant 9 ("every crossing declares its loss") in its own words.

## Extension: n + k

Fourteen cases where the marked extra is a block rather than a unit: the seven planets completed to nine by two *invisible* bodies — Rāhu and Ketu in India, and the Taoist Dipper's '九星，今星七見，二隱不出' (nine stars, seven seen, two hidden; Tao Hongjing) 🟢 — a structural parallel between two traditions that reach 9 from 7 with two unseen members; the three stop codons over the 61 sense codons; the Septuagint's seventy-two 'called the Seventy'; the conscripti beside the patres; sikidy's four columns generating sixteen; the Ogham's fifth aicme of five forfeda; the Hebrew and Greek numeral alphabets adding 5 and 3 letters to reach 27; the sixteen cowries plus two; the mahjong set's eight bonus tiles; the Goetic knights over the 72. They confirm the marking without confirming the unit step, and are counted separately.
