## What would break the framework

1. A tradition that closes at n and crosses at n + 2 with no marked n + 1 and no return. (The fourteen *extension* cases — n + k with k > 1 — are all *marked* blocks and all keep a unit crossing elsewhere in the same tradition; they are not counterexamples, but they are the nearest thing to one and are counted separately.)
2. A positional or fractional scribal culture whose sacred and calendrical numbers are systematically irregular.
3. A base-60 or unit-fraction culture that puts 7 on order and 6 on chaos systematically. (The census in 05 measures the current registry: P(7 | passage) = 0.28 vs P(7 | order) = 0.18, ratio 1.61, permutation p = 0.008; the controls 9 and 13 do not separate.)
4. A closed hull in a flood or passage narrative that is developable (a rolled sheet) rather than polyhedral or sealed.
5. Plato's *Timaeus* with two elementary triangles per face rather than six (he rejects two explicitly at 54e).
6. An independent 360 reached by a factorization not among the twelve divisor pairs.
7. **New in Ω2:** a divination device whose set-aside unit is *counted* — a rosary whose head bead is a bead, a yarrow rite that reads with fifty, an Ifá set that casts the seventeenth nut.
8. **New in Ω2:** an "uninvited one" myth in which the excluded element's exclusion has no consequence.

## The null model, stated

With enough numbers in a tradition, some n and some n + 1 will both occur. Three disciplines keep the framework from being numerology:

- **Marking.** A crossing is entered only when the source marks the element (hidden, uncounted, unnumbered, uninvited, unlucky, holy, forbidden, witness, gate). Bare adjacency is [N].
- **Seat typing.** Each crossing must be one of five seats or the typed exception, and the seat must be readable from the source's own description. A crossing with no seat is not a crossing.
- **The census.** Role labels are assigned per number before counting. The ratio P(7 | passage) / P(7 | order) can only fall under adversarial relabelling; at 1.61 on 521 numbers, with a permutation p of 0.008 and null controls at 9 and 13 that do not separate, it is the framework's one quantitative claim about numbers, and it is reproducible from the registry with `seed = 0`.
- **The schema.** `closure_grammar_schema.validate_registry` refuses a 🟢 crossing without a locus or a sourced tradition, refuses unmarked adjacency, and refuses a unit seat that is not n+1. The report will not render on a registry that fails it.
