# ATHENA UNIFIED V7 — AΩR × COLLECTIVE DUAL-CONTROL OPERATING SPEC

## 0. Scope

This specification binds the verified AOR developmental cortex to Collective Runtime V1–V7 without collapsing model output, organizational memory, evidence, authority, execution, or persistence into one scalar state.

The governing braid is:

`AOR = WHAT is developmentally eligible`

`COLLECTIVE = HOW scarce execution / science / control capacity is organized`

`Y1 = canonical claim authority`

`OMEGA = current accessible whole-state projection`

`CYCLE = resumable developmental metabolism`

## 2. Canonical claim namespace law

Two claim systems coexist and MUST NOT share RPC names.

Canonical Y1 authority:

- `athena_claim_register`
- `athena_claim_state`
- `athena_claim_promote`
- `athena_claim_challenge`
- `athena_claim_resolve_canonical_challenge`

V6/V7 science-shadow replication/falsification:

- `athena_discovery_claim_register`
- `athena_discovery_claim_witness`
- `athena_discovery_claim_state`

A science-shadow state such as `REPLICATED_SUPPORT`, `FALSIFICATION_SIGNAL`, or `CONTESTED` is evidence-navigation metadata. It cannot implicitly promote, demote, challenge, or canonicalize Y1.

`SCIENCE_SHADOW --X--> IMPLICIT_Y1_MUTATION`

Any model/shadow output that should affect Y1 must cross an explicit witnessed evidence/authority route.

## 3. V7 operator family

### 3.1 Uncertainty decomposition

`athena_uncertainty_decompose` exposes diagnostic proxies such as aleatoric-noise, parameter-uncertainty, calibration, and drift/OOD contributions.

These components are model-conditional diagnostics:

`UNCERTAINTY_PROXY != PHYSICAL_UNCERTAINTY_DECOMPOSITION`.

### 3.2 Prequential interval

`athena_prequential_interval` uses historical pre-update predictive residuals. When there are too few scores it returns an insufficiency state rather than inventing a band.

A prequential band is not a distribution-free coverage guarantee without the assumptions required for such a guarantee.

### 3.3 Causal skeleton discovery

`athena_causal_skeleton_discover` may surface a heuristic association/partial-correlation skeleton for hypothesis generation.

`HEURISTIC_ASSOCIATION_SKELETON != CAUSAL_DAG`.

The output cannot enter Y1 as causal evidence without a separately justified identification/test route.

### 3.4 State-dependent transition model

`athena_state_transition_model` reconstructs action-conditioned local transition behavior only from observed transition data. An unseen action remains `UNSEEN_ACTION`.

Prediction, scenario evaluation, and planning MUST NOT self-create transition observations.

### 3.5 Scenario evaluation

`athena_scenario_evaluate` is finite model simulation and returns a simulation-only decision surface.

`SIMULATE_ONLY != OBSERVED_FUTURE`.

### 3.6 Dual-control proxy

`athena_dual_control_plan` balances expected reward, model risk, novelty/information leverage, and learned transition state in a bounded planning surface.

Its authority is:

`DUAL_CONTROL_PROXY_PLAN_ONLY`.

Operational law:

`PLAN -> EXECUTE_ONLY_FIRST_AUTHORIZED_ACTION -> OBSERVE_REALITY -> REPLAN`.

The planner is not a semantic executor and never promotes its own simulated trajectory into observed state.

### 3.7 Extended causal identification

`athena_causal_identify_extended` supports conditional identification surfaces including back-door, front-door, and instrument-style criteria under caller-supplied graph/observability/assumption semantics.

An identification result means:

`IDENTIFIED_UNDER_SUPPLIED_MODEL`.

It does not mean unconditional causal truth in the world.

### 3.8 Replication independence geometry

`athena_replication_independence` computes diversity/effective-sample diagnostics over explicit witness metadata such as dataset, implementation, method, operator, environment, and seed family.

Its effective-N output is a diversity diagnostic, not a proof that witness processes are statistically independent.

### 3.9 Replication design

`athena_replication_design` may rank future replication/falsifier candidates by novelty, expected power, feasibility, cost, and falsifier value.

Authority:

`DESIGN_ONLY`.

The selected experiment is not itself a replication or falsification result.

## 4. AOR → Collective V7 execution law

AOR remains upstream of resource allocation.

1. FIELD emits explicit candidate work.
2. Measurement/calibration resolves required AOR operands.
3. Y1/dependency/test/persistence gates determine eligibility.
4. AOR chooses a structured successor frontier.
5. Collective receives only eligible work through typed transport.
6. Collective allocation/science/control measurements remain distinct from AOR value/evidence.
7. V3–V7 models may choose how to measure, schedule, simulate, or experiment.
8. A real executor must provide an execution receipt.
9. A witnessed test must verify the outcome.
10. Only observed/witnessed outcomes update learning/reward/authority surfaces.

## 5. No self-training from model output

The following are forbidden feedback shortcuts:

- predicted RGO -> observed RGO;
- counterfactual score -> empirical reward;
- transition prediction -> transition observation;
- rollout/scenario -> observed trajectory;
- dual-control plan -> executed action;
- replication design -> replication witness;
- discovery shadow -> Y1 authority.

The general rule is:

`MODEL_OUTPUT --X--> OBSERVATION_WITHOUT_EXTERNAL_WITNESS`.

Topology→JSPACE projection remains a recovery saga rather than a falsely atomic SQLite+Git transaction.

## 10. Residual research boundary

The unified V7 runtime does not claim:

- exact general Bayesian belief-state dual control;
- universal nonlinear posterior inference;
- causal graph truth from heuristic skeletons;
- assumption-free front-door/instrument identification;
- formal replication independence from metadata diversity alone;
- automatic authority promotion from model/science-shadow state;
- generic semantic execution without an actual executor;
- distributed atomic commit across semantic SQLite and Git.

Those remain explicit successor work, not silently renamed complete.
